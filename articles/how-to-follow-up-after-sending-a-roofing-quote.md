# How to Follow Up After Sending a Roofing Quote

## Quick answer

Follow up on a roofing quote after 24 hours, again after 3 days, and once more after 7 days if appropriate. Keep the message helpful, not pushy. Ask if the customer has questions, timing concerns, or needs help choosing the next step.

## Why quote follow-up matters

Roofing quotes are often high-value decisions. Customers may be comparing options, waiting on insurance, discussing budget, or simply busy.

If you do not follow up, another contractor can win the job.

## Recommended follow-up schedule

### 24 hours after quote

Goal: answer questions and keep momentum.

Message:

Hi {{First Name}}, just checking in on the roofing estimate we sent.

Do you have any questions, or would you like help choosing the next step?

### 3 days after quote

Goal: understand hesitation.

Message:

Hi {{First Name}}, following up on your roofing estimate.

If timing or budget changed, no problem. Should we keep this open for you, or would you like to review any part of the quote?

### 7 days after quote

Goal: final polite check-in.

Message:

Hi {{First Name}}, I wanted to check once more on your roofing estimate.

Would you like us to keep this active, or should I close the file for now?

## What to track

Track:

- quote sent date
- quote amount
- follow-up dates
- customer objections
- outcome
- next step

## Free tools

- Google Sheets for quote tracking
- Gmail templates for messages
- Google Calendar for reminders
- Tally Free for estimate requests

## Mistakes to avoid

- Sounding desperate
- Offering discounts too early
- Forgetting to record objections
- Sending too many messages
- Not following up at all

## Final recommendation

A consistent quote follow-up system can be more valuable than chasing more leads. Start simple and make sure every quote has a next step.
