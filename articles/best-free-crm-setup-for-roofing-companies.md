# Best Free CRM Setup for Roofing Companies

## Quick answer

The best free CRM setup for a small roofing company is usually a simple Google Sheets or HubSpot Free pipeline that tracks every lead from first contact to quote follow-up and review request. Do not start by buying complicated software. Start by fixing the follow-up process.

## Who this is for

- Roofing companies with 1–30 employees
- Owners still tracking leads in email, phone notes, or memory
- Office managers who need a cleaner daily lead workflow

## The problem

Most roofing companies do not lose leads because they lack software. They lose leads because there is no single place to see:

- who requested an estimate
- who was contacted
- who received a quote
- who needs follow-up
- who became a customer
- who should be asked for a review

A free CRM setup should solve that first.

## Recommended free setup

Start with either:

1. **Google Sheets** — easiest and free
2. **HubSpot Free CRM** — better if you want a real pipeline

For the first version, Google Sheets is enough.

## CRM fields to include

- Lead ID
- Date received
- Name
- Phone
- Email
- City/service area
- Service type
- Urgency
- Lead source
- Status
- Owner
- Last contacted
- Follow-up date
- Quote sent
- Quote amount
- Outcome
- Review requested
- Notes

## Best statuses

Use these stages:

1. New Lead
2. Contacted
3. Estimate Scheduled
4. Quote Sent
5. Needs Follow-Up
6. Won
7. Lost
8. Review Requested

## Simple daily workflow

Every morning:

- Check all new leads
- Contact leads not reached yet
- Follow up on quotes from yesterday
- Follow up again after 3–7 days
- Ask completed customers for reviews

## Recommended tools

### Free starting point

- Google Sheets
- Tally Free for estimate forms
- Gmail templates
- Google Calendar

### Upgrade path

- HubSpot Free/Starter
- Jobber
- Housecall Pro
- Review management software

## Common mistakes

- Buying software before defining statuses
- Letting leads live in email only
- Not assigning an owner
- Not setting follow-up dates
- Forgetting review requests

## Final recommendation

Start with a free lead tracker first. Once the team uses it consistently, then compare paid CRM or field service tools.
