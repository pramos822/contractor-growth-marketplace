Lead leak score

A stronger visual makes the quiz feel like a useful diagnostic, not a generic form.

Answer 8 quick questions to estimate whether leads may be leaking from your follow-up process.

1. Is your phone number visible immediately on mobile?
2. Do you have a clear “Request Estimate” call-to-action?
3. Does your form ask for service type, urgency, and preferred contact method?
4. Are new leads logged in one place?
5. Does your team respond to most new leads within 15 minutes?
6. Do you follow up on quotes after 24 hours and 3 days?
7. Do you request reviews after completed jobs?
8. Does someone own daily lead follow-up?
Note: This quiz is a diagnostic tool, not a guarantee. Use it to start improving your follow-up process.
