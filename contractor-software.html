Software guide

Contractor software should fix a workflow, not create another one.

Workflow first

Choose software that fixes a real process before adding more features.

Use this guide to choose software by operational stage instead of chasing random features.

Stage 1: free workflow

  • Google Sheets lead tracker
  • Tally estimate request form
  • Gmail templates
  • Google Calendar follow-up reminders

Best when the team is still proving its follow-up process.

Stage 2: CRM and scheduling

  • HubSpot CRM for general pipeline tracking
  • Jobber or Housecall Pro for home service operations
  • Calendly for estimate bookings

Best when leads are coming in consistently and manual tracking is slowing the team down.

Stage 3: reviews and automation

  • Podium/Birdeye for reputation workflows
  • Zapier/Make for workflow automation
  • n8n for technical self-hosted workflows

Best after the team has a clear process and wants to reduce repeated admin work.
